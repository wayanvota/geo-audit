Methodology built into the prompt

Crawl Access

Checks robots.txt, sitemap discovery, AI crawler policy, HTTPS, redirects, noindex, canonical tags, and visible HTML.

Citability

Scores direct answers, definitions, FAQs, numbered structure, source links, author signals, and evidence quality.

Entity Clarity

Finds conflicting claims across pages, schema, social profiles, directories, and AI-generated brand summaries.

User-Referenced Retrieval

Tests what AI tools retrieve, cite, and get wrong when a user names the site or provides its URL.

Competitor Benchmark

Compares mention rate, citation rate, share of voice, cited URLs, content gaps, and source-page quality.

Measurement

Separates signal health from output performance, then defines monthly prompt checks and quarterly full audits.
