Visibility risk

For Nonprofit Staff

Nonprofit communications and development teams need to know what AI assistants say when a donor, journalist, partner, or board member asks about their organization.

Common failure AI tools miss current programs, repeat stale leadership, cite old directories, or recommend better-described peer organizations.
Preset focus Mission, programs, impact evidence, donation routes, trust signals, peer comparisons, and source-of-truth pages.
Accuracy risk

For Foundation Staff

Foundations have a different risk: AI assistants may misstate funding priorities, geography, grant size, exclusions, deadlines, or application routes.

Common failure Grantseekers waste time, program staff absorb avoidable inquiries, and public guidance looks inconsistent.
Preset focus Eligibility, exclusions, current programs, source documents, applicant questions, and false-fit risk.
Resource allocation

Why I Built It

Wayan Vota builds free tools for social-impact teams because bad information wastes scarce money, staff time, attention, and trust. GEO Audit Studio is meant to improve resource allocation: donors should find organizations that actually match their intent, nonprofits should not lose visibility because their facts are hard to parse, and grantseekers should not chase foundations based on inaccurate AI summaries.

What it tests What public AI systems retrieve, cite, summarize, miss, and get wrong.
What it avoids Claims about private AI rankings, hidden model weights, or unsupported visibility scores.
What owners get A repeatable way to find weak source pages and decide what to fix first.
